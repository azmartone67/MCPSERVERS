# MCPSERVERS

## DC Hub Intelligence

**[DC Hub Intelligence](https://github.com/azmartone67/dchub-mcp-server)**: live, cited data center, power grid, interconnection, fiber, gas and site-selection intelligence as a remote MCP server with 94 tools.

- **Remote MCP endpoint:** `https://dchub.cloud/mcp` (Streamable HTTP)
- **Connect guide:** https://dchub.cloud/connect-mcp (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Grok and other MCP clients)
- **Tools:** 94
- **Coverage:** a global data center facility map, 170+ countries, 300+ markets and 1,700+ tracked deals
- **What it answers:** market intelligence and rankings, power availability and interconnection queues, energy and gas prices, fiber and peering, M&A and hyperscaler deals, site analysis and scoring, water, climate and disaster risk, tax incentives and permitting
- **Access:** works without a key in preview mode; `claim_free_key` returns a free key in one call. Plans and trial: https://dchub.cloud/pricing
